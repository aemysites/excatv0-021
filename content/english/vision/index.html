KDDI Group Philosophy

The KDDI Group Philosophy represents the employees' way of thinking, values, and the code of conduct. This philosophy includes our ideals and the thoughts to be respected in KDDI as a primary telecommunications carrier. The KDDI Group Philosophy is not only the basic attitude that KDDI employees should take, but it is also the foundation of our CSR. Through the practice of this philosophy, we aim to be a company trusted and cherished by all stakeholders.

Chapter 1: Vision

  • Enhancing the power to connect
  • Satisfying customers
  • Keeping aspirations alive
  • Delivering a thrilling customer experience by always going further than expected
  • Making the most of diversity
  • Responsibility extending 24/7 and 365 days a year
  • Striving to become a truly global company
  • Each one of us makes the KDDI Group what it is

Chapter 2: Management Principles

  • Clearly state the purpose and mission of our business
  • Respect for human rights
  • Pursue profit fairly
  • Transparent management
  • Maximizing revenues, minimizing expenses
  • Lean and mean management
  • Real-time business management

Chapter 3: Professional Mindset

We set specific goals with a pure and aspirational mind. Once our targets are set, we will drive ourselves to keep going with a strong commitment until we achieve success. Then we will share the fruits of our accomplishments.

Chapter 4: Way Forward

  • Do the right thing as a human being
  • Go back to the basic principles
  • Playing by the rules
  • Separate personal matters from business
  • Praise each other's challenges
  • Conceive optimistically, plan pessimistically, and execute optimistically
  • Every small effort counts
  • Believe in our potential
  • Always be creative in your work
  • Open and direct communication
  • Taking ownership
  • Be self-motivated
  • Keep your fighting spirit alive
  • Let's unite as a team to achieve our goals
  • Look at what you do, from the outside in
  • To know the problem, go, see and touch
  • Be agile; think and act with a sense of urgency
  • Put yourself in the perspective of your boss
  • Tough love at work

Chapter 5: Elevate Our Minds

  • Achievement = Attitude x Effort x Ability
  • Refine the human skills through work
  • Be altruistic
  • Be grateful to others
  • Have an open mind and a humble attitude
  • Be positive

KDDI Code of Business Conduct

KDDI formulated the KDDI Code of Business Conduct in 2003 as specific guidelines for understanding and practicing the corporate principles and the KDDI Group Philosophy. All KDDI officers and employees abide by this Code of Business Conduct to maintain high ethics in the course of their everyday duties. In April 2016, this Code of Business Conduct was revised for the purpose of responding to new establishments, amendments, and abolishments of laws and regulations, as well as changes in social conditions, and promoting group management. The codes of conduct of individual group companies are also established and revised as needed considering the their business characteristics, but still in reference to the KDDI Code of Business Conduct.
